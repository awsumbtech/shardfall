# Shardfall v0.3 — "The Wider Hush" (shared spec for all lanes)

Owner: Brian (plays on a Samsung S25 Ultra, portrait, touch). Personal, ad-free game. Plain HTML/JS/canvas, no build step, no network assets, GitHub Pages from main. Keep Reduce Flashing (default on), save/load, and 60fps on a phone. Every lane commits this file as `docs/v0.3-spec.md` (identical copy; if it already exists on main, leave it).

## The big change
Shardfall becomes a **turn-based, Final Fantasy-style RPG**: you explore the islands in real time (walk, dash, grapple stay), but **fights happen on a separate battle screen** with a party, commands, levels, gear, and items. The old real-time strike combat is removed. The art moves to a **16-bit SNES-era pixel look** (think FF6 / Chrono Trigger), drawn in code.

## HARD RULE: dialogue
Brian approves every spoken line before it ships. **No lane writes new spoken dialogue into the game.** New NPCs and scenes use node ids that point at a placeholder: `startDialogue('saltmere.xyz')` with a single line `"(Script pending)"` and speaker name. The STORY lane writes the Act 2 script as a document only. Short system text (menus, item descriptions, battle log like "Kestrel attacks!", "Wisp is defeated.", "Level up!") is fine and must be plain and literal. No riddles, no metaphor-y flavor text.

## Shared contracts

### Battle API (BATTLE implements; WORLD calls)
- `Battle.start(encounterId, { boss?:bool, canFlee?:bool, bg?:string, onWin?:fn, onLose?:fn })`
- Field enemies are visible on the map (no random encounters). Touching one starts its encounter. Winning removes it (saved). Losing returns you to the last lamp/waking stones with party at half HP, nothing lost. Boss fights: `canFlee:false`.
- `bg` ids: `emberhold_meadow`, `emberhold_scar`, `cinderveil_ash`, `ember_vault`, `saltmere_shore`, `saltmere_wreck`, `highspire_road`, `hush_deck` (fights on the skiff).
- Encounter ids (BATTLE defines contents, WORLD places them): `em_wisps`, `em_brumekin`, `em_choirling`, `cv_ashlings`, `cv_cinderhound`, `cv_mixed`, `boss_cinder_maw`, `sm_saltwisps`, `sm_drowned`, `sm_gulls`, `sm_mixed`, `boss_tidewyrm` (Saltmere boss, placeholder-fight is fine), `hs_choir_patrol`, `hs_sentinel`, `sea_hush_raiders`.

### Battle design (BATTLE)
- Turn order by Speed, shown as a turn-order strip at the top (FFX-style CTB). Clear and phone-friendly.
- Party up to 3 on screen. Members: **Wayfarer** (shard magic, balanced), **Kestrel Vane** (she/her, skiff captain, quick, pistol and knife, steals), **Tamsin Reed** (engineer, gadgets, buffs, heals with tonics). Kestrel joins when you first sail (`party_kestrel` flag), Tamsin joins after the grapple gift (`party_tamsin` flag). Join is a plain banner ("Kestrel joined the party."), no dialogue.
- Commands: Attack, Skill, Item, Defend, Flee. Big buttons at the bottom of the screen, thumb-reachable. Targeting by tapping enemies or party portraits.
- Stats: HP, SP (skill points), Attack, Defense, Magic, Speed. Elements: Ember, Tide, Gale, Shard. Enemies have weaknesses shown after one Scan or one hit.
- Each character learns skills by level (about 6 each through level 20). Wayfarer examples: Shard Bolt, Mend, Lampflare (all-enemy, Shard). Kestrel: Quickshot, Steal, Twin Cut. Tamsin: Spark Coil, Patch Up, Overclock (speed buff).
- Leveling: XP shared to all party members, curve to level 30, stat growth per character, "Level up!" results screen with stat gains and new skills. Gold ("marks") and drops.
- Items: Tonic, Hi-Tonic, Ether, Phoenix Shard (revive), Smoke Bomb (flee), Ember Salt, etc. Equipment slots: Weapon, Armor, Charm, with clear stat numbers. Shops use item ids from `js/data/items.js`.
- Field menu (pause button): Party / Status, Skills, Items, Equip, Save, Settings. FF-style blue-glass windows (ART styles them).
- Boss: Cinder Maw becomes a turn-based boss with phases (targets its glowing core). The shard-in-the-core beat and Act 1 story events stay the same.
- Rebalance Act 1 so a normal playthrough reaches around level 8 by the Maw.
- Victory fanfare, battle music via existing `Music.play('battle')` / `Music.play('boss')` (add a `battle` track) and Sfx. Haptic tap on hits.
- Save migration: v0.2 saves load into v0.3 with a sensible level from old XP and the right party members from story flags.

### Art API (ART implements; others call, with fallbacks if missing)
- Pixel art defined in code (palette-indexed string grids or similar) and pre-rendered to offscreen canvases at load, drawn with `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` and integer scaling. No downloaded images.
- `Art.sprite(ctx, id, x, y, { frame, facing, scale })` — field and battle sprites. Ids: `wayfarer`, `kestrel`, `tamsin`, `maren`, `hesk`, `ilsa`, `cantor`, `villager_a`, `villager_b`, enemies `wisp`, `brumekin`, `choirling`, `ashling`, `cinderhound`, `cinder_maw` (large), `saltwisp`, `drowned`, `stormgull`, `tidewyrm` (large), `choir_soldier`, `sentinel`, `hush_raider`, objects `chest`, `chest_open`, `lamp_cold`, `lamp_lit`, `shard`, `skiff`, `cog`, `sign`.
- `Art.portrait(ctx, id, x, y, size)` — dialogue/menu faces for named characters.
- `Art.battleBg(ctx, bgId, w, h, t)` — one per bg id above.
- `Art.window(ctx, x, y, w, h, opts)` — FF-style menu window (deep blue gradient, light border, rounded). Also CSS equivalents for DOM menus.
- `Art.tile(...)`/terrain: restyle islands, cliffs, grass, paths, water/Hush, buildings, trees, rocks into a cohesive pixel tileset look. Keep the dusk sky and cloud sea identity.
- Walk animations (4 directions, 2–4 frames) for party and NPCs; idle/attack/hurt/cast frames for battle sprites.

### World expansion (WORLD)
- A **world map / sail chart** screen: pick a destination from the islands you've unlocked, with a short sailing scene. Sea fights can happen on the skiff (`sea_hush_raiders`, bg `hush_deck`) sometimes.
- New islands:
  - **Saltmere** — a fishing town on stilts over the Hush whose lamp is failing. Town (shop, inn to rest and save, NPCs), the Shore, the Sunken Wreck (small dungeon with a grapple puzzle and chests), boss arena for `boss_tidewyrm`. Unlocks after Act 1 ends.
  - **Highspire outskirts** — the road up to the Lanternwright city: gate town with a shop and inn, the switchback road with encounters, gate locked ("Story continues in Act 2") for now. Unlocks after Act 1 ends.
  - Two small optional islets reachable by sail: **Gullrock** (treasure, tough optional fight) and **The Drift Market** (a floating trader with rare gear).
- Expand Emberhold and Cinderveil a bit: more paths, chests with items/gear, an inn/rest point on each, Lowmoor Quay shop.
- Encounter zones: visible enemies placed in sensible spots per island, respawn when you rest at an inn (not on every reload).
- Treasure chests (saved), shops (buy/sell), inns (rest = full heal + save + respawn field enemies).
- All new NPCs get placeholder dialogue ids (see hard rule). Name them in a list in the PR so STORY can write their lines.

### Act 2 script (STORY)
- `docs/script/act2.md`: same format and plain style as `docs/script/act1.md` (read it first, it is the approved bar). Story picks up from the Act 1 ending (Highspire as the destination; Saltmere's failing lamp if you chose Listen, discovered anyway otherwise). Covers Saltmere's arc and the arrival at Highspire's gate. Includes party join beats for Kestrel and Tamsin, short battle victory lines per character, shop/inn keeper lines, and lines for every NPC WORLD adds (use the NPC list in this spec: Saltmere has a harbormaster, an inn keeper, a shopkeeper, a lamp keeper who is sick, two kids, a fisher; Highspire gate has a guard captain, a shopkeeper, an inn keeper, a Choir sympathizer; the Drift Market trader).
- Natural, plain speech. Distinct voices. Tells a clear story. No riddles, aphorisms, or "clever" mystical lines. Kestrel is she/her.
