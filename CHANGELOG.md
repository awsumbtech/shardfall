# Changelog

Notes for someone playing, not a patch log of every file.

## v0.3.4 — Install the game

Shardfall can be installed, like an app, from the field menu: More, then Install.
On a phone it also installs from the browser menu. On a computer, use the install icon in the address bar.
Once installed it opens full screen, sideways, and it keeps working with the network off.
A newer version replaces the old one the next time you open it.

## v0.3.1 — Room to read

The phone screen is less cramped. Heart, level, shards, and the quest share one short card. A place name fades in, then leaves. The map is smaller until you tap it. Tips no longer mention keyboard keys on a phone.
In a fight the people and the foes are easier to see and to tap. A skill or item list sits along the bottom, and the heart stays on screen. Sideways, the commands sit on the right.
The menu opens on Party, Items, Equip, Skills, Save, and Resume. The rest is under More. The page can be added to a phone's home screen.

## v0.3 — The Wider Hush

Fights are turn-based. Walk into a foe, or press Fight when one is close.
Attack, Skill, Item, Defend, and Flee sit along the bottom of the screen. The turn order is the strip at the top.
Act 1 is fought alone, through Emberhold, Cinderveil, and the Cinder Maw.
Kestrel and Tamsin join after the act ends. The banners say "Kestrel joined the party." and "Tamsin joined the party."
Levels go to 30. Marks are the coins. The pause button opens Party, Skills, Items, Equip, Save, and Settings.
The Cinder Maw is fought in turns. Aim at the core.
A saved game from v0.2 keeps going, with a level from the old journey. A finished Act 1 adds both companions. A journey still on the road stays solo.
The sail chart opens after Act 1. Saltmere, Gullrock, the Drift Market, and the road to Highspire are on it.
Saltmere is a stilt town: Quinn's Hooks, the Stilthouse, and the Holdfast under the west pier. The gap to the wreck takes a hook.
Lowgate sits under the city. You can shop and sleep there. The gate stays shut until Saltmere's lamp is lit, and the city is still closed after that.
Chests remember when you open them. Shops buy and sell. A night at an inn fills your heart and brings field enemies back. A boss stays down.
Reduce flashing stays on until you turn it off.
In a fight the people and the foes are larger, and the turn strip shows each foe's face. An attack steps in, a hit knocks back, and Ember, Tide, Gale, and Shard leave a small colored mark with a damage number. A win raises a victory pose. None of that flashes white. The objective and the hint share one box, and a hint fades after the first time you see it.

The islands, the people, and the fight are drawn in a 16-bit pixel style.
The Wayfarer carries a glowing shard. Kestrel wears a coat and goggles. Tamsin has a tool belt. Maren is old. Ilsa wears armor. Hesk wears Choir robes. The Cantor wears a pale mask.
Saltmere, the Holdfast, Lowgate, Gullrock, and the Drift Market use that same pixel style. The sail chart, the chests, the shops, and the inns do too. Brenna Holt, Osric Pell, Nessa Quinn, Sera Dunne, Pip, Lark, Jory Hale, Warden Brant, Dorrie Fen, Maud Keel, Ellis Ward, and Sile Voss each have a sprite and a portrait.
The dusk sky and the crack in it stay.
Open the page with ?gallery to look at every sprite, portrait, and battle background.

## v0.2.1 — The Cantor walks up

Act 1 is told again, in the words of the people on the road.
After the Cinder Maw, the Cantor comes up the ridge and waits until you answer. Spare them, hear them out, or refuse.
Kestrel is at the gangplank when you get back to Lowmoor Quay. Ahead lies Highspire, and the last of the Lanternwrights.
The scene no longer stops while the Cantor is still walking.

## v0.2 — Act 1: The Cinder Road

The Quickmercy sails. Cinderveil is open: Lowmoor Quay, the Ashfields, and the Ember Vault.
Tamsin fits a grapple to your hand. Hook the gaps, pull the small things, and haul yourself at the big ones.
Three cogs, Ilsa's trial, then the Cinder Maw. The hurt core takes three shards. What you ask is remembered, and the Cantor waits at the end.

The title opens on a cloud sea, islands drifting past a lamp.
Emberhold has a quiet theme now. A strike, a dash, a shard, and the lamp each have a sound, and each person has a voice when they talk.
Music and effects have their own volume. Haptics can be turned off. The same Reduce flashing switch lives in Settings.
Sound waits for the first tap, and it rests when the phone sleeps.

## v0.1.1 — Steady light

The screen stays steady. Flashes are softer, and Reduce flashing is on until you turn it off.
Your next step stays on screen. The button changes to Talk, Place shards, or Inspect when you are close enough.
A marker points the way. The first minute teaches the buttons once, and a missed gap says to try Dash.

## v0.1 — Emberhold

Emberhold is awake: wander the isle, dash across the broken span to Cinderstep, and cross the rope bridge to Kestrel's skiff.
Strike the Hushborn, gather five sky-shards, and relight Maren's great lamp.
Your reward is Shardstep — a farther, faster dash — and a rumor of the next island, Cinderveil.
